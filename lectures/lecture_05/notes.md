## Slide 8 — Marshall–Lerner gives a local condition

At balanced initial trade, the first-order NX effect equals initial trade value times (epsilon_X+epsilon_M−1) times the proportional real depreciation. The condition is local and depends on supply/pricing assumptions. With an initial trade imbalance, the elasticities receive different value weights. Do not present the unweighted condition as universally sufficient.

## Slide 11 — The dollar can matter in third-country trade

Neither seller nor buyer needs to be American for dollar invoicing to transmit a dollar shock. In the dominant-currency paradigm, dollar exchange rates can drive short-run import prices and volumes more than bilateral exporter-importer rates. This is a conditional pricing mechanism, not a claim that all trade is invoiced in dollars.

## Slide 37 — Counterfactual exercise: who can ease?
