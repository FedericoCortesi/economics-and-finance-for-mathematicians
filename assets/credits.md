# Teaching illustrations

Original illustrations generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this course. They depict invented examples, not real businesses, institutions, historical documents or observed events. They contain no empirical data. LaTeX chart/diagram sources provide the economic labels.

- `bakery.png`: a farmer, mill and bakery in one continuous editorial scene, used for value added.
- `trade.png`: an invented factory, port and retailer, used for trade and imported-input exposure.
- `gold.png`: an invented bank counter exchanging paper claims for gold, used for convertibility.
- `money_bakery.png`: an invented bank loan, oven investment and bread purchase, generated 15 October 2026 for Lecture 2 with the built-in image-generation tool.

Prompts requested a wide 16:9 painted/cut-paper editorial style, navy/teal/ochre on ivory, no text, no numerical data, no logos and clear physical teaching examples. Full generation prompts are retained in `../docs/image_prompts.md` alongside the course source files. No textbook cartoons or photographed slide pages were reused.
